# Vendor copy contract — the locked-offer banner

**Hand this page to iGoDirect.** It specifies the wording their portal must render when a
Tools Australia member opens an offer above their access level.

## Why we supply this string

The banner is customer-facing copy **about Tools Australia's own membership packs**, rendered
by a third party. Two rules apply to it that iGoDirect has no reason to know, and both are
legal exposure for us, not style preferences:

1. **No gambling framing.** Tools Australia runs an Australian game-of-chance **trade
   promotion**, not gambling. The words **odds, chance(s), lottery, lotto, raffle,
   sweepstake, gamble, bet** must never appear in copy about our product.
2. **Entries are never sold.** Members buy a **membership or a pack**; the free entries come
   *with* it. Copy must never describe entries as something purchased, and must never price
   them per unit. The banner therefore does not mention entries at all — its subject is
   catalogue access.

The current banner happens to be clean. Nothing keeps it clean through the vendor's next
content edit, which is the whole reason this contract exists.

## The string

Generated by `buildVendorLockedOfferCopy()` in
[`src/utils/partner-discounts/tier-upgrade-copy.ts`](../../src/utils/partner-discounts/tier-upgrade-copy.ts).
The same module drives our own catalogue page, so the two surfaces cannot drift.

**Headline**

```
This offer is above your current access level.
```

**Body** — two variants; pick by whether a membership tier covers the offer.

```
Your membership opens {openCount} of {totalCount} partner offers.
{tierName} opens {tierCount} — including this one.
```

```
Your membership opens {openCount} of {totalCount} partner offers.
A one-time pack opens more of the catalogue.
```

**Button**

```
Upgrade to {tierName}          (or "See packages" when no tier covers it)
```

**Link**

```
https://toolsaustralia.com.au/membership
  ?utm_source=partner_portal
  &utm_medium=referral
  &utm_campaign=rewards-return
  &offer_id={offerId}
```

`offer_id` is **required**: it is what lets our page name the offer the member was refused
instead of showing a generic pitch. We already parse it (`resolvePortalReturn`).

## Worked example — a Tradie (50%) opening a 100% offer

> **This offer is above your current access level.**
> Your membership opens 917 of 1,833 partner offers. Boss opens 1,833 — including this one.
> **[ Upgrade to Boss ]**

## Placeholders

| Token | Meaning | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `{openCount}` | offers the member's current level opens | our tier table |
| `{totalCount}` | offers in the catalogue (1,833 today) | our tier table |
| `{tierName}` | cheapest membership covering this offer | Tradie / Foreman / Boss |
| `{tierCount}` | offers that tier opens | our tier table |
| `{offerId}` | the offer the member was refused | vendor |

Counts change when the catalogue changes. If the vendor prefers fixed strings over
placeholders, we will supply refreshed values on request — but **the wording above is not
theirs to edit**. Any change comes back to us first.

## What the banner must not do

- Must not use the banned vocabulary in §1, in any tense or compound.
- Must not mention draw entries, or imply entries are bought.
- Must not name a price. Prices change; the link goes to the live page.
- Must not say "catalog" — Australian English, **catalogue**.
- Must not render on an offer the member **can** use.
- Must not be the only exit: the offer page still needs a working back path.

## Related asks

This contract assumes the fixes in
[`igodirect-portal-ux-audit.md`](igodirect-portal-ux-audit.md) — in particular **ask 1**
(mark locked offers on the card, not only after a click), **ask 5** (suppress merchant copy
and contact details on a locked page) and **ask 6** (price the upgrade). The banner is what a
member sees *after* they have already been let down by the unmarked card; fixing the card
matters more than perfecting this string.
